My Journey

Author of

"Bal Bagaicha"

Anenthusiastic and deeply devoted soul, I weave my life through the powerful disciplines of justice, literature, and active reform. Guided by my unwavering faith as a Krishna Bhakt, I channel my spiritual strength into tangible actions for my country, Nepal.

As a dedicated LLB student at Prithvi Narayan Campus, Tribhuvan University, I am preparing myself to be a voice of justice. I believe the law is a vital instrument for healing society and defending the defenseless.

"A fierce advocate for change, a voice for the voiceless, and a guardian of my homeland's environment."

Beyond the courtroom, I am a poet (कवियत्री) and the author of "Bal Bagaicha". Through the beautiful Nepali language, I write verses that capture the essence of life, struggle, and profound beauty.

My words are matched only by my actions. I am a social worker and reformer. During the Nepal Gen-Z Revolution, I stood boldly on the frontlines. Today, I continue to organize rallies to protest injustices in my local area and spearhead vital environmental cleanup campaigns to preserve the natural beauty of my homeland.
